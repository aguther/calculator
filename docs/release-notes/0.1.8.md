## Detect updates without fully restarting the PWA

- The PWA checks for new versions when returning to the app, reconnecting, and every 60 seconds while visible.
- Update detection resumes on reconnection even after an offline startup.
- A new version is offered only after it has loaded completely. Switching versions still requires the update button.

## Clearer touch feedback

- Keys immediately depress and change contrast when touched.
- Even very short touches are shown; feedback remains briefly after release.
- Multiple fingers and canceled touches remain correctly handled. Animation does not change hit targets.

## First update note

Updating from 0.1.7 may require fully closing and reopening the app once more. Improved update detection takes effect from 0.1.8. Existing calculations and preferences are retained.

The web app and Windows/Linux packages were updated.
